Download

Download Wayzerr

Free. One team of bots on your desk, whatever computer you use. By downloading or using Wayzerr you agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

First time opening it?

Mac: right-click Wayzerr, choose Open, then Open again.

Windows: double-click the downloaded Wayzerr file (if it doesn’t end in .exe, rename the end to .exe) and wait a minute while it installs. If it says the app is unrecognised, press More info, then Run anyway. Prefer no installer? Download the zip, unzip it and open Wayzerr.exe.

Linux: double-click the downloaded Wayzerr file and press Install, or run sudo apt install ./Wayzerr.deb. Another kind of Linux? Get the AppImage (or the ARM64 AppImage for ARM computers such as a Raspberry Pi), run chmod +x Wayzerr.AppImage, then open it.

Two extras you add when you want them

The app itself stays small. When you first open it, Wayzerr offers these and you choose what to add.

About 1 GB download, onceA computer for each bot

The compressed size; it holds about 3 GB of software once unpacked. Every bot gets its own Linux computer from this one download. For Apple silicon Macs.

Why you would want it: a bot can install and run programs, download and test apps, and finish jobs that need a real desktop, even when a search or the browser can’t. It can’t touch your files, only the shared Wayzerr Files folder.

0 MB to downloadA browser

It is built into Wayzerr, so turning it on adds nothing to the download. It keeps what you sign in to, usually 20 to 60 MB.

Why you would want it: it starts instantly, so bots can read pages, click through sites, fill in forms and sign in with your saved logins without waiting for a computer to start.

FAQs

Wayzerr gives you AI bots, each with its own personality, memory and tools. Ask them questions, or have them research, write, plan, and use the web and your apps for you. Your chats stay on your computer.

Each bot has its own personality and memory, so you stop explaining yourself every time. It uses the AI plan you already pay for, has its own computer (a small Linux desktop that runs only inside Wayzerr; on Windows and Linux it still has its own browser), and keeps your chats on your computer. You can give one bot a job each: homework, your inbox, the news.

Wayzerr itself is free. The AI behind your bots is what costs money, and that depends on how you connect it: a plan you already have (Cloud) costs nothing extra, and an API key is charged by the company that gave you the key.

Open Settings → AI & apps. Under Cloud, sign in with a Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or other plan you already pay for. No key needed. Under API, paste a key from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and others. You can connect several and pick one for each bot with the model button next to the message box.

Cloud uses a plan you already have, so there's nothing extra to pay. An API key charges per use, gives you more models and is needed for good pictures. Start with Cloud and add an API key later if you want one.

Chats, memories and saved logins stay on your computer, and logins are encrypted. Your messages go only to the AI you picked. Your Wayzerr account is just your email address, and Wayzerr sends no usage statistics. See our Privacy Policy. Details are in Settings → General → Privacy.

Press the + at the top of the sidebar. Give the bot a name and a short description (they decide who it is), pick its look and create it. For a group, choose New group in the same place and pick the bots. They can talk to each other.

Yes. Open a bot, press the monitor button, and choose the Schedule tab. Press New task, say what the bot should do, then pick the date, time and how often (once, every day, every week and more). Press Open calendar for the full week view: drag a bot onto any time to schedule it, and open Run logs to see what happened. You can also just tell a bot in the chat ("every morning at 8, read me the news") and it sets it up. Wayzerr has to be open at that time; it keeps the computer awake while tasks are waiting.

Every message re-sends the whole chat plus the AI's own built-in instructions, and a long chat keeps growing. Click the bot's name and press Compact chat to shrink it. A blue dot shows when it's worth doing. In Settings → General, AI usage set to Low saves the most.

Open Settings → AI & apps → Connectors, paste your free Composio key once (you make it at dashboard.composio.dev), then press Connect on an app. Or tell a bot "use your computer" and it works in its own computer instead.
