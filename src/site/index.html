Download

Download Wayzerr

Free. One team of bots on your desk, whatever computer you use.

First time opening it?

Mac: open the downloaded Wayzerr.dmg and drag Wayzerr into Applications. Then right-click Wayzerr, choose Open, then Open again.

Windows: double-click the downloaded Wayzerr-Setup.exe and wait a minute while it installs. If it says the app is unrecognised, press More info, then Run anyway. Prefer no installer? Download the zip, unzip it and open Wayzerr.exe.

Linux: double-click the downloaded Wayzerr.deb and press Install, or run sudo apt install ./Wayzerr.deb. Another kind of Linux? Get the AppImage (or the ARM64 AppImage for ARM computers such as a Raspberry Pi), make it runnable with chmod +x, then open it.

You need: a Mac with macOS 13 or newer, a Windows 10 or 11 PC (64-bit), or a 64-bit Linux computer. More detail is on the download page.

Two extras you add when you want them

The app itself stays small. When you first open it, Wayzerr offers these and you choose what to add.

530 MB to 1 GB download, onceA computer for each bot

The compressed size: about 1 GB on a Mac, about 580 MB on Windows and about 530 MB on Linux. Wayzerr shows the exact size before it downloads anything. Every bot gets its own Linux computer from this one download. It works on Apple silicon Macs, Windows PCs and Linux computers; an Intel Mac uses the browser instead.

Good to know: on Windows it runs fastest when “Windows Hypervisor Platform” is turned on in Windows Features. On Linux it needs QEMU (and KVM for full speed), and Wayzerr tells you what to install.

Why you would want it: a bot can install and run programs, download and test apps, and finish jobs that need a real desktop, even when a search or the browser can’t. It can’t touch your files, only the shared Wayzerr Files folder.

0 MB to downloadA browser

It is built into Wayzerr, so turning it on adds nothing to the download. It keeps what you sign in to, usually 20 to 60 MB.

Why you would want it: it starts instantly, so bots can read pages, click through sites, fill in forms and sign in with your saved logins without waiting for a computer to start.

Wayzerr itself is free. The AI behind your bots is what costs money, and that depends on how you connect it: a plan you already have (Cloud) costs nothing extra, and an API key is charged by the company that gave you the key.

Open Settings → AI & apps. Under Cloud, sign in with a Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or other plan you already pay for. No key needed. Under API, paste a key from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and others. You can connect several and pick one for each bot with the model button next to the message box.

Cloud uses a plan you already have, so there's nothing extra to pay. An API key charges per use, gives you more models and is needed for good pictures. Start with Cloud and add an API key later if you want one.

Chats, memories and saved logins stay on your computer, and logins are encrypted. Your messages go only to the AI you picked. Wayzerr asks you to sign in with an email address, and that account is only your email. It sends no usage statistics. Details are in Settings → General → Privacy.
