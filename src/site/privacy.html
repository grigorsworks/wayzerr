3. Sensitive features

Bots can use saved logins and cards and, on a Mac, control your computer, but only if you allow it. Saved passwords and cards are encrypted on your computer and are not shown to bots. Anything a bot types into a website or sends to an AI provider goes where you told it to go. Don't put information in chats that you don't want the chosen AI provider to receive.

4. Why we use information, and our legal bases (EU/UK)

  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse (legitimate interests).
  • To answer your questions and comply with the law (legitimate interests, legal obligation).
  • Optional features such as the free picture service or online icons run only if left switched on (consent, which you can withdraw in Settings).

We don't sell personal information or share it for cross-context advertising. We don't make decisions about you that have legal effects using automated processing.
