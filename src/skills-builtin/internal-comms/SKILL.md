# Internal communications
Lead with what changed and what the reader must do (and by when). Then why, then detail. Plain words, short paragraphs, bullets for lists, no hype. Status updates: done, doing, blocked, next. Incidents: what happened, impact, cause, fix, prevention. End with who to ask.
