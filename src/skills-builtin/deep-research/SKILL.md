# Deep research
1. Split the question into 3 to 6 sub-questions. 2. For each, search several different sources (official sites, primary data, reputable press); open the pages, do not rely on snippets. 3. Note date and source for every fact; prefer recent; flag disagreements. 4. Write the report: answer first (3 sentences), then sections per sub-question, a short comparison table when options exist, then sources as links. 5. Say what you could not confirm. Never invent figures or quotes.
