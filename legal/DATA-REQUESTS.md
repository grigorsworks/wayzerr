# Handling privacy requests

Covers access, deletion, correction, export, objection and withdrawal of consent (GDPR/UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and similar).

1. **Receive** at hello@wayzerr.com (or privacy@). Log: date received, requester, type, deadline.
2. **Verify identity.** Ask them to write from the account email, or sign in and confirm a code. Never act on a request from a different address without extra proof. Don't ask for ID unless really necessary.
3. **Deadline:** reply within 30 days (GDPR: one month; CCPA: 45 days). Acknowledge within 5 working days.
4. **Do it:**
   - *Access/export:* send the data in a readable file: email, sign-up date, devices, tunnel name, terms version accepted.
   - *Deletion:* call the deletion process (BACKEND-REQUIREMENTS.md #2), then remove from email lists, support mail, backups at next rotation. Tell them chats and keys on their own computer are removed from the app via Delete account.
   - *Correction:* update the email, send confirmation to both addresses.
5. **Reply** with what you did, what you couldn't do and why (legal duty to keep, etc.), and that they may complain to their data protection authority.
6. **Record** the outcome and keep the log 3 years, without the data itself.

Third parties (AI providers, Composio) hold their own copies; point the person to them for requests about data sent there.
