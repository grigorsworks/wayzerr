How to open Wayzerr the first time

1. Drag Wayzerr onto Applications.
2. Open Wayzerr. macOS may say it can't check it for malicious software. Click Done (not "Move to Trash").
3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway" next to Wayzerr, and confirm.

You only do this once. (On older macOS you can also right-click Wayzerr > Open > Open.)

Then sign in with your email, take the short tour and make your first bot. Pick how your bots think: sign in with the Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan you already have, or paste an API key.
