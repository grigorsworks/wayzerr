# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want Wayzerr's community to be a harassment-free, welcoming place for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour
- Be respectful and constructive; give and accept feedback graciously.
- Assume good faith and focus on what is best for the project.
- Respect other people's time, privacy and boundaries.

## Unacceptable behaviour
- Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information (such as an address or email) without permission.
- Spam, or posting malware or harmful links.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope
This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, the website) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement
Report problems to **hello@wayzerr.com**. We will review every report promptly and fairly, keep the reporter's identity private, and may remove comments, lock threads, or ban people who break these rules.
